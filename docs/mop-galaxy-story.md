# Mop & Galaxy — story canon

Approved by Sipes, 2026-10-04. Nothing is implemented yet; the home page lists it as "In development, coming 2027". The implementation spec is `docs/mop-galaxy-design.md`. Game id `mop-galaxy`; SKUs `mop-galaxy` (full game) and `mop-galaxy-walkthrough`.

## Premise
Wim Tarragon, Custodial Technician Third Class, is the only person awake aboard the colony ship *Luminous Hyacinth*, fourteen months out from Earth with 2,000 colonists and a 30-person crew in cryosleep. Custodians are contractors, not crew, so nobody froze him. His only company is MOP-7 ("Mop"), a floor-polishing robot with a maintenance sub-mind, who answered the airlock because a ship called the *Lien* said it had a delivery. The delivery was a boarding party.

## The hook
The Vellacourt Reclamation Fleet exploits a loophole in deep-space salvage law: a vessel with no conscious crew aboard is derelict, and derelicts can be claimed. Captain Thessaly Vane has filed the claim with MOP's signature as "ship's representative" and is towing the *Hyacinth* to the Vellacourt yards, where frozen colonists are worth more as cargo than as people. The one flaw in the claim is a conscious man in overalls. Vane's crew have one job before they reach the yard: put the janitor to sleep.

## Cast
- **Wim Tarragon** — hero. Custodial Technician Third Class. Contractor badge, not crew. Knows the ship by its ducts, chutes and closets.
- **MOP-7 / "Mop"** — floor-polishing robot, cheerful, literal, no sense of consequence. Narrates deaths with sincere admiration. Signed the salvage papers. Companion throughout; a hotspot in most rooms.
- **Captain Thessaly Vane** — commander of the salvage ship *Lien*. Precise, legalistic, never raises her voice. Keeps the *Hyacinth* captain's thaw code in her breast pocket.
- **Captain Adaeze Okonjo** — captain of the *Luminous Hyacinth*, frozen until chapter 8. First line on waking: "Who are you?"
- **Thistle** — hydroponics bot. Grumpy. Controls food and the tool locker. Won't serve contractors.
- **Gumbo** — fist-sized stowaway blob the colonists' children smuggled aboard. Eats plastic. Grows. Loyal to whoever feeds it.
- **Vane's crew** — Brack (big, kind, bored), Ilse (engineer), Dorrit (the one who reads the regulations aloud).
- **Lane Authority** — deep-space law enforcement; arrives in the epilogue.

## Ships
- ***Luminous Hyacinth*** — colony ship. Decks: 9 Custodial (closets, laundry chutes, service ducts), Cryo Bay, Galley & Hydroponics, Engineering (jump drive, coolant loop, reactor), Hull (comms dish), Bridge.
- ***Lien*** — Vellacourt salvage ship, docked to the *Hyacinth* by a collar amidships. Holds full of salvage from other "derelicts". Vane's quarters. Docking clamp control.

## Summary
Wim's shift from hell. Locked out of every system but his own, he works through the ship the way a custodian knows it. He proves to Thistle he counts as crew, befriends Gumbo, fouls the jump drive's coolant loop with floor polish and oven cleaner to buy twelve hours, goes outside in a borrowed suit to point the comms dish at a Lane Authority relay, and confronts Vane on the bridge, where he learns the thaw code is in her pocket. He boards the *Lien*, lifts the code while Mop polishes the corridors into a skating rink, blows the docking clamps, and wakes Captain Okonjo four minutes before the ship crosses into Vellacourt jurisdiction. The epilogue promotes him to Custodial Technician Second Class, which comes with a slightly better mop.

## Chapters
1. **Deck 9, Custodial** (free scene) — Wim wakes in a supply closet to alarms and Mop's news that a delivery has arrived, watches armed strangers through a floor vent, and has to get out of a janitorial deck the hijackers have sealed from the outside.
2. **The Cryo Bay** — Among two thousand sleeping colonists and a frozen captain, Wim finds Vane's salvage declaration pinned to the bay door, learns that his being awake is the only thing voiding it, and hides from the first search party inside an empty pod.
3. **Galley and Hydroponics** — Thistle won't release food or tools to a contractor, so Wim has to prove he's crew, befriends Gumbo, and leaves with a toolkit and a very hungry ally.
4. **Engineering** — With Vane's engineers rerouting the jump drive to the Vellacourt yards, Wim uses the only chemicals he has clearance for to foul the coolant loop, buys the ship twelve hours, and loses his mop to an airlock.
5. **The Hull** — In a borrowed suit, Wim goes outside to re-aim the comms dish at a Lane Authority relay and broadcast that the *Hyacinth* has a conscious crew member aboard, using Mop's polish canister as a thruster.
6. **The Bridge** — Face to face with Vane, Wim learns that Mop signed the salvage papers, that his own contract calls him a contractor, and that the captain's thaw code is in Vane's breast pocket.
7. **The *Lien*** — Wim boards the salvage ship through the docking collar, works through holds full of other ships' stolen lives, lifts the thaw code from Vane's quarters while Mop turns the corridors into a skating rink, and blows the docking clamps.
8. **Thaw** — Wim wakes Captain Okonjo with four minutes to spare, the Lane Authority arrives to a bridge full of hijackers sliding into the brig, and the first conversation Wim has ever had with his captain ends in a promotion and a better mop.

## Tone
Space Quest underdog slapstick. Airlocks, vending machines, the reactor and Thistle's compost unit can all kill Wim; every death is a one-liner, mostly delivered by Mop. 250 points, 2–3 hours, ages 12+ (milder than Port Lucky; no alcohol gags).
