# Last Night in Port Lucky — story canon

Approved by Sipes, 2026-10-04. Chapters 1–2 are already implemented in the demo (`public/index.html`); everything here is consistent with that code. The implementation spec for chapters 3–8 is `docs/port-lucky-design.md`.

## Facts fixed by the demo
- Hero: Dex Morrow, best man. Wakes 9:47 AM, Honeymoon Suite (Suite 702), Palmetto Royale, Port Lucky, Florida. Hawaiian shirt, one-lens sunglasses, marker on arm: "DON'T LET BENNY NEAR THE GOAT".
- Groom: Benny, Dex's oldest friend. Wedding at 4:00 PM.
- Suite: goat in bow tie on green sofa (chewing keycard), tuba with valet ticket No. 42 (3:12 AM, "NEVER AGAIN" on the back), fizzing blue cocktail (fatal), minibar with $38 Crackers of Regret, flamingo painting with lipstick moustache, disco ball, phone marked "DO NOT CALL MOM". Front desk: Benny "took the car"; the goat is not included in the room rate.
- Garage P2: Kevin the teenage valet; Mr. Sprinkles, a pink ice-cream truck Dex arrived in at 3 AM and played the jingle in for an hour; inside: one of Benny's size-11 tuxedo shoes and a receipt: BIG EARL'S PAWN & KARAOKE, 4:02 AM, 1 x wedding ring (pawned), 6 x karaoke tokens. Demo ends 10:31 AM.
- Score 250. Length 2–3 hours. Ages 16+. Sierra-style deaths with one-line epitaphs.

## Cast (new)
- **Lucy Hartwell** — the bride. Knows about the goat plan all along (revealed in ch. 8).
- **Dolores Hartwell** — mother of the bride; runs the Hartwell family seaside inn. The "lady in the lilac hat" who bought the ring back from Earl at 9 AM. Withholds it until Dex tells the whole truth.
- **Benny Vance** — the groom. Pawned the ring to enter Earl's all-night karaoke tournament whose prize was the goat; won; found the ring already sold; left on a pontoon in one shoe "to think".
- **Gus** — the goat. Beloved mascot of the Hartwell inn, won off Dolores in a card game by Big Earl years ago. Hates brass instruments. Walks the ring down the aisle.
- **Big Earl** — owner of Big Earl's Pawn & Karaoke (never closes). Turns up at the wedding to repossess his mascot.
- **Kevin** — valet (existing).
- **Sal** — real owner of Mr. Sprinkles; traded the truck for Dex's watch; wants both back.
- **Captain Marguerite** — charter captain of the party pontoon.

## Summary
A six-hour countdown told as a reverse treasure hunt. Dex reconstructs a stag night nobody remembers by following its debris across town: suite → garage → Big Earl's → boardwalk → marina → Hartwell suite → pavilion. The night was less reckless than it looks: Benny pawned Lucy's grandmother's ring to win back the Hartwell family goat, the one gesture he thought could win over the bride's mother. Dolores recognised the ring in Earl's window, bought it at 9 AM, and spends the day watching whether the best man will own up. Dex gets the ring only by telling the truth, rescues Benny from a drifting pontoon with a tuba, a jingle and a goat, and gets everyone to the altar with twelve minutes to spare. Lucy knew. Gus was included in the room rate after all.

## Chapters
1. **The Honeymoon Suite** (9:47 AM, free scene) — Dex wakes among the wreckage, bribes the goat with crackers, fishes a valet ticket out of a tuba, and learns from the front desk that Benny "took the car."
2. **Level P2** (10:15 AM) — Kevin trades the ticket for the keys to Mr. Sprinkles; the truck gives up Benny's shoe and the pawn receipt revealing the ring is gone.
3. **Big Earl's Pawn & Karaoke** (11:00 AM) — Dex out-sings a hungover regular to get Earl talking and learns Benny pawned the ring to win the goat, which was sold on at 9 AM to a lady in a lilac hat.
4. **The Boardwalk** (12:15 PM) — Working the pier's arcade, fortune teller and churro stand, Dex buys back his wallet, phone and dignity piece by piece and strikes a deal with Sal.
5. **The Marina** (1:30 PM) — Dex spots the pontoon drifting past the breakwater with Benny asleep aboard and improvises a rescue from a tuba, a jingle, a goat and Captain Marguerite's grudging help.
6. **The Hartwell Suite** (2:40 PM) — Dolores has had the ring since breakfast; Dex can lie, charm or bargain, but only the whole embarrassing truth gets it out of her handbag.
7. **The Seaside Pavilion** (3:48 PM) — With the clock on screen, Dex juggles a one-shoed groom, a tuxedo, a goat, a vengeful pawnbroker and the groom's mother to reach the altar by four.
8. **"To the Happy Couple"** (4:00 PM, epilogue) — Dex delivers a best man's speech assembled from the day's clues, Gus carries the ring, Lucy reveals she knew about the goat plan, and the final score tallies how much of last night Dex pieced together.

## Hazards (deaths)
Blue cocktail (ch. 1), the breakwater (ch. 5), Earl's karaoke machine (ch. 3), the tuba (various). Every death is a one-liner with Try again / Restart scene.
